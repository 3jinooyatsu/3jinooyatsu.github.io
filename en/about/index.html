AboutWho we are

Products that bring a little breathing room to every day.

Mission

You could get by without it. But with it, your day gets a little better. That is the kind of product we want to make, like a snack at three in the afternoon. We plan, build and deliver our own products, all in-house.

What we learn about design and engineering from our own products goes straight into the apps we build for clients. We don't stop at launch. We stay with a product until people keep coming back to it. A small team, working with care.

Our Name

Why “3jino oyatsu”?

You could get by without it.
But with it, your day gets a little better.

In Japanese, 3時のおやつ (3jino oyatsu) means “the three o'clock snack”: a short break in the afternoon to put things down and catch your breath.
We chose the name because we want to make things that quietly keep you company in the same way, every day.
